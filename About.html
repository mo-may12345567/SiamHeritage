"Why create this site?"

keeping the tradition alive

Thai arts and crafts have been shaped by generations of artisans who transformed natural materials into objects of beauty and meaning.

These beautiful traditional crafts connect the present with Thailand's cultural past.

We want people around the globe to explore the beauty of Thai crafts, this helps people know more about Thai tradition!
